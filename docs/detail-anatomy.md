# Details beyond the heart

Explore details now uses named structures and bones in the selected area, with laterality preserved. Examples include eyes and kidneys, liver and brain surfaces, lung lobes, stomach regions, pancreas regions, knee structures, skull bones, ribs, hand bones and foot bones. Areas with only one named structure continue to support isolated inspection without an empty detail picker.

The picker magnifies a structure without recording it. Record finding on this structure explicitly adds it to the assessment. Bones retain the existing `bone`/`boneName` contract; tissues use `part`/`partName`. Whole organ / Whole area returns to the prior view and restores layer preferences. Read-only review supports inspection without modifying the assessment.

New named subdivisions are schematic documentation anchors. Their positions and shapes are simplified, and are not patient-specific imaging or a diagnostic anatomical atlas.

Labels were checked against OpenStax Anatomy and Physiology 2e: [lung lobes](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/22-2-the-lungs), [stomach regions](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-4-the-stomach), [pancreas regions](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/23-6-accessory-organs-in-digestion-the-liver-pancreas-and-gallbladder), and [knee menisci and cruciate ligaments](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/9-6-anatomy-of-selected-synovial-joints). The right lung includes a middle lobe; the left lung picker omits it.
